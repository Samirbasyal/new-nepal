Member panel redesign, and any email can now be typed for a duty

ROLE DUTIES - THE EMAIL FIELD
- The member email was a fixed dropdown, so an address that had already been
  saved (Samir - samirbasyal81@gmail.com) could not be replaced. It is now a
  plain email box: type any address, replace the old one, save.
- The server no longer refuses an address that has no account yet. The duty is
  saved with the email as typed and left unlinked; the member panel matches on
  the email as well as on the linked account, so the duty appears the moment
  that person's account exists.
- Editing an existing row re-links it when the address matches an account.

MEMBER PANEL - NEW SHELL
- Desktop: a dark green menu column (logo, grouped links, mountain foot) beside
  the page, each link with its own badge for unread alerts and payments waiting.
- Phone/tablet: a slim top bar with the logo, bell (unread count), avatar and
  sign out; a five-button bar pinned to the bottom (Home, Tasks, Fund, Alerts,
  Profile) with safe-area padding so it clears the phone's gesture area.
- The page area scrolls on its own; the menu and the bars stay put.

MEMBER DASHBOARD - NEW CONTENT
- Greeting strip with the member's first name, post and membership status.
- Four cards: open tasks this month, permanent responsibilities, fund status for
  the month, unread notifications - each linking to its page.
- Performance card with a ring gauge, score, rank and the committee's remarks,
  or a plain note when nothing has been published yet.
- Quick access grid to every panel page, then the latest notices, programmes and
  events.
